# Image credits

The hero is composited from real photographs of model and high-power rocketry.
No stock-agency imagery, no orbital launch vehicles, and nothing generated.

Every layer — plate, airframe, exhaust plume, smoke — is matted from the sources
below by `scripts/mat_assets.py`, which is re-runnable and documents exactly what
was done to each one.

## Plate (ground, treeline, foreground), airframe, plume, smoke puffs

**"Backlit Beauty"** by Steve Jurvetson (Flickr: `jurvetson`)
Licensed **CC BY 2.0** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Backlit_Beauty_(10412761253).jpg

Modifications: rocket, exhaust plume, pad smoke, power lines and pole removed to
produce a clean "waiting" plate; airframe, plume and six smoke puffs extracted as
separate alpha-matted sprites; whole frame regraded (graduated ND, desaturation,
filmic curve, vignette).

## Sky

**U.S. Department of Defense photograph by Cherie A. Thurlby**, 2007 Team America
Rocketry Challenge national finals, The Plains, Virginia.
**Public domain** (work of the U.S. federal government).
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Defense.gov_photo_essay_070519-D-7203T-003.jpg

Modifications: sky band extracted, rescaled so its horizon registers with the
plate's treeline, and regraded to match.
