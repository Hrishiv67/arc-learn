## Make it official before liftoff

A good practice flight cannot become a qualifying flight afterward. Arrange an eligible NAR observer and declare the attempt **before ignition**.

## Prepare with your adviser

- Check the current rules, deadlines, and official flight report together.
- Confirm the observer meets the independence requirements and arrange the two independent timers.
- Inspect the rocket and payload before the attempt. Keep the team's entry number on the rocket.
- After recovery, remove the eggs and altimeter in the observer's presence. Do not conceal damage or change a reading.

Every declared attempt must be reported using the required process, including a disqualification. The form explains which reports the team submits and which the observer sends. The full rules explain replacement-flight exceptions; ask the observer rather than guessing.

## Show how you learned

**Mission Debriefing:** explain your design, test results, and the decisions those results changed. Keep evidence from the start.

**Rocket Reels:** tell a clear story about your team. Check the rules and judging rubric; use only music and images you have permission to include.

**Keep going:** explore the events, video lessons, and optional team activities in the resource library. For finals, check the announced targets again—they can differ from qualification.
