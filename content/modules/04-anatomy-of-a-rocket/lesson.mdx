## Outside: shape and structure

**<GlossaryTerm id="nose-cone">Nose cone</GlossaryTerm>:** front cover that guides air around the rocket. **<GlossaryTerm id="body-tube">Body tube</GlossaryTerm>:** holds the parts together. **<GlossaryTerm id="fins">Fins</GlossaryTerm>:** help the rocket stay pointed along its flight path.

A **coupler** joins matching tubes; a **transition** joins different diameters. **Rail guides** slide along the launcher while the rocket builds speed.

## Inside: push, measure, protect

| Part                          | What it does                                               |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Motor                         | Makes thrust; a suitable ejection charge releases recovery |
| Motor mount + centering rings | Hold the motor straight inside the larger tube             |
| Motor retainer                | Stops the motor falling or ejecting out                    |
| Egg bay                       | Cushions the payload and opens for inspection              |
| Altimeter                     | Measures height using changing air pressure                |

The <GlossaryTerm id="altimeter">altimeter</GlossaryTerm> needs outside air through correctly sized vents. Secure it so it cannot hit an egg. Follow its mounting instructions and check the current approved list in the rules.

## Recovery: bring every part back

The **<GlossaryTerm id="parachute">parachute</GlossaryTerm>** slows descent. The **shock cord** connects sections after they separate. Flame-resistant wadding or a suitable **<GlossaryTerm id="nomex">Nomex</GlossaryTerm> blanket** protects recovery gear from hot ejection gases. Pack it according to its instructions so the chute can open freely.

**Memory trick:** body holds, motor pushes, fins steady, chute slows, padding protects.

<Callout title="Find the failure" tone="info">
  A rocket returns with a scorched parachute. Check heat protection and packing
  first. A sealed altimeter bay is a different problem: it affects the height
  reading.
</Callout>
