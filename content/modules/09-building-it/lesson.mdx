## Dry-fit before glue

Lay out the parts and fit them together without adhesive. Check that tubes, couplers, motor mount, and retainer match. Leave room to remove the eggs and altimeter.

## Build straight and secure

- **Measure and mark:** use a guide to mark equal fin positions. Cut with adult supervision and the right eye protection.
- **Align:** keep fins straight along the tube and evenly spaced. Hold parts still while the adhesive cures.
- **Bond:** use an adhesive suited to the materials. Follow its preparation, ventilation, and full curing instructions.
- **Reinforce:** a _fillet_ is a smooth bead of adhesive along a joint. It spreads load at the fin root; a giant glue blob mainly adds weight.
- **Retain:** a hook, clip, or screw-on retainer must keep the motor in place. Friction alone is not enough for ARC.

## Check before closing the airframe

Trace every recovery attachment. Confirm that the chute can leave its bay and that the nose or separating section moves freely. Follow the altimeter manufacturer's vent-hole instructions; a sealed bay cannot track outside pressure.

Weigh the finished rocket and update the simulation. If you print a part, test a sample and review its strength with your mentor; appearance alone cannot prove it will survive flight.
