## Give every person a job

Assign a checklist reader, rocket preparer, data recorder, and flight watcher. The launch crew controls the pad and countdown. Bring your notebook, approved equipment, and spare recovery supplies.

## Before the pad

1. **Site:** the crew confirms permission, clear airspace, suitable weather, and safe ground conditions.
2. **Rocket:** inspect fins, joints, retention, and recovery connections. Confirm the loaded mass and balance match the checked design.
3. **Payload:** secure the eggs and altimeter; confirm the altimeter is ready using its instructions.
4. **Recovery:** verify heat protection, untangled lines, and a free-moving separation joint.
5. **Record:** note the motor and delay, mass, weather, and your prediction before the flight.

## At launch

Follow the range officer's instructions. Keep people behind the safety line. Stop for a person or aircraft entering the area, a new gust, damaged hardware, or anything unclear. Anyone may speak up.

## After landing

Wait for permission to recover. Never catch the rocket. Photograph damage before changing anything and write down height, duration, egg condition, and what you saw. On an official attempt, present the payload and altimeter to the observer as required.

</Callout>
