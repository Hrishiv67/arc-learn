## Your team has one job

Build a rocket that repeats a good flight: the right height, the right time, and unbroken eggs.

## Split the work, share the knowledge

- **Design:** draw the rocket and simulate it.
- **Build:** measure, assemble, and check each part.
- **Test:** keep the checklist, weather notes, and flight results.
- **Coordinate:** track parts, budget, meetings, and deadlines.

Rotate jobs. Everyone should be able to explain the rocket. Adults teach skills and supervise safety; students design, build, and fly the competition entry.

## Work backward from launch day

1. **Learn:** try a basic practice kit with your mentor. It is for learning, not your competition entry.
2. **Design:** compare a few ideas on paper and in software before buying parts.
3. **Build:** leave time for glue to cure and mistakes to be fixed.
4. **Test:** book several flying days, including backup days for weather.
5. **Qualify:** arrange an eligible observer before the deadline rush.

Keep one shared notebook: sketch → prediction → flight result → next change. Add photos of the build so you can explain decisions later.
