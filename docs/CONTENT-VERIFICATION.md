# Course content update

Reviewed 2026-09-20/21. The file-based MDX lessons remain the website content source.

## Content decisions

- All 13 modules now have short readings and application quizzes. Most readings are about two minutes; the challenge overview is longer and includes a scoring explorer. Existing learner progress is preserved.
- Full 2027 rules sections 4.3–4.6 and the parameters sheet take precedence over the starter kit, which still contains older targets and conflicting dates. Annual values live in content/seasons/2027.ts.
- Corrected the previous F-only motor claim, cotton-only parachute claim, constant body diameter, missing mass limit, cracked-egg scoring, and the claim that a larger chute lands harder.
- The supplied folder has 22 resource entry pages. All are represented in content/resources.ts, including optional videos, community links, and team extras. Video pages are optional references; the lessons do not claim to transcribe unseen videos.
- The parts slides, flight-testing guide, rules, parameters, handbook, qualification form, starter kit, design article, and optional challenge documents were downloaded and text-inspected. Source files stay in ignored artifacts/sources. Older examples are labeled; conflicting Mission Debriefing dates are flagged rather than repeated as reliable deadlines.
- Physical building and launching remain adult-supervised. Passing the safety quiz unlocks those readings, not permission to operate a launch site.

## Images

Unmodified NASA Glenn educational GIFs are stored in public/images/learning and linked to the original explanatory page beside each diagram. NASA's media guidelines permit factual educational use with acknowledgement; no endorsement is implied. Existing user-supplied component photographs remain in the anatomy lesson. The simple flight graph is illustrative, not invented measurement data.
